Welcome to Meow Match!

🐱 😺 😸

Match 3 or more cats in a row to score points!

How to Play:

  • Tap a cat, then tap an adjacent cat to swap
  • Or swipe to swap cats
  • Match 3+ identical cats to score
  • Match 4 cats = 📦 Box power!
  • Match 5 cats = 🍣 Tuna power!
  • Match 6+ cats = 🎰 Slot Machine power!
  • Fill a row AND a column = 🪄 Cat Wand! (swap any 2 cats free)
  • Cascades double your points every chain!
  • Follow-up cascade matches earn powers too!
  • Powers carry over between levels!
  • Reach the goal score to advance
  • Beat 28 levels to face the FINAL BOSS!
  • Points add to your total at the end of every level!
  • Jot down your save code to pick up where you left off!

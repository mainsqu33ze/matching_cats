Welcome to Meow Match!

🐱 😺 😸

Match 3 or more cats in a row to score points—the bigger the chain, the bigger the payoff!

Rules

  • Tap a cat, then tap an adjacent cat to swap (or swipe)
  • Match 3+ identical cats to score points
  • Cascades double your points every chain—and earn powers too
  • Powers carry over between levels!
  • Reach the goal score AND finish the level's objectives to advance

Powers

  • Match 4 cats = 📦 Box power!
  • Match 5+ cats = 🍣 Tuna power!
  • Fill a row AND a column = 🪄 Cat Wand! (swap any 2 cats free)
  • Score an 8x combo = 🎰 Slot Machine power!

Special Tiles

  • ❄ Ice & 📦 crates melt when you match beside them
  • 🪨 Rocks need a power or bomb blast to break!
  • 💣 Countdown bombs tick down every turn—match them to defuse!
  • Later levels add quota colors, 💎 deliveries, 👶 babies & runner cats!

Progress

  • Points add to your total at the end of every level
  • Beat 28 levels to face the FINAL BOSS!
  • Jot down your save code to pick up where you left off!
